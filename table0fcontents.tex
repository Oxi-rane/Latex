### The important commands

```latex
\tableofcontents
```

→ creates the **Table of Contents**

```latex
\listoffigures
```

→ creates the **List of Figures**

```latex
\listoftables
```

→ creates the **List of Tables**

```latex
\index{Machine Learning}
```

→ adds **Machine Learning** to the index

```latex
\printindex
```

→ prints the index

And these two are required for the index:

```latex
\usepackage{makeidx}
\makeindex
```

The `\caption{...}` commands are what make figures/tables appear in their respective lists.
