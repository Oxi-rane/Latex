\section{Introduction}

Computer science covers many areas such as algorithms, artificial
intelligence, databases, and operating systems.

Algorithms are fundamental to computer science \cite{cormen}.
Artificial intelligence is discussed in detail in \cite{russell}.
Database systems are covered in \cite{silberschatz}.
